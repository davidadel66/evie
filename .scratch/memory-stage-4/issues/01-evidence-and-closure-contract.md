## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Define the initial useful fact classes and selection rubric, distinguishing required useful memories, permitted optional suggestions, unsupported suggestions, and unwanted but true information. Preserve original episodes and operational Task/domain authorities.
- [ ] Choose a minimal initial named tool-observation contract with explicit eligible capabilities, completed outcomes, fields, original authority, and freshness meaning. Undefined tool output remains ineligible.
- [ ] Specify direct owner assertions, quotations, reported speech, hypotheticals, and bounded assistant interpretation context. Role labels and matching quotations are insufficient to establish semantic entailment.
- [ ] Define exact field/range coordinates, Unicode handling, permitted structured-field projection and hashing, multi-source attribution, and identical projection for validation, review rendering, and accepted-source inspection.
- [ ] Define bounded windows, overlap ownership, newly covered evidence versus interpretation context, oversized-input behavior, scope lineage, and cross-session boundaries. Shared scope alone cannot combine unrelated conversations.
- [ ] Provide an eligibility/closure matrix and worked histories for successful, failed, cancelled/interrupted, crashed, command-only, and incomplete-tool sequences. Committed eligible evidence remains available without inventing terminal events after lease loss.
- [ ] Specify secret exclusion and source-data treatment for embedded instructions. Exclude reasoning, opaque continuation state, snapshots, summaries, retrieved blocks, compiler outputs, and diagnostics as supporting evidence.
- [ ] Record scope and explicit Promotion invariants. Resolve material unanswered choices with the owner and record binding decisions; unresolved choices must remain visible blockers rather than implementation assumptions.
- [ ] Verify worked examples against the current event and semantic contracts, check documentation whitespace and local links, and identify the deterministic fixture cases that dependent tickets must implement.
