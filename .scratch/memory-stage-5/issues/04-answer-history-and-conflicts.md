## What to build

Evie distinguishes what was said then from what is accepted now, including newer conflicting statements.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Carry explicit current/historical intent and supported Valid/Transaction Time constraints through accepted-memory and conversation retrieval, preserving unknown dates instead of inventing precision.
- [ ] Allow explicit historical questions to retrieve retired corresponding evidence with retired status marked; ordinary search remains unable to retrieve it. Historical intent never restores source access or broadens scope.
- [ ] Supply contradictory active Claims and relevant newer owner excerpts with their authority and timestamps. Demonstrate Boston in saved memory versus a later Chicago statement without silently overwriting either.
- [ ] Preserve tentative trip wording, quoted speech, and assistant-inference authority; newer text alone is not stronger truth. Unsupported answers identify their uncertainty.
- [ ] Render historical/retired state and conflicts through existing source inspection and activity presentation while retaining the original evidence receipt.
- [ ] Use deterministic fixtures to verify actual supplied evidence, time boundaries, source exclusions, and absence of writes; use separately pinned model-backed cases for attributed historical answers and discrepancy handling. Run required verification.
